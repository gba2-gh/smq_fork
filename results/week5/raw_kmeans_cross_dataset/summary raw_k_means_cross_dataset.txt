This freezes the HuGaDB recipe—raw windows, K-means-512, the reference contextualizer, guard 0 and 5,000 updates—and fits the pipeline separately on:
- HuGaDB.
- BABEL-1, BABEL-2 and BABEL-3.
- LARa, with global normalization and an additional per-recording normalization condition.
Each condition uses three seeds. This tests transfer of the configuration; it does not apply one HuGaDB-trained model unchanged to other datasets.
The completed rkx_v2 reports:
Dataset / normalization	Selected-checkpoint F1@50	Locally rescored SMQ
HuGaDB	46.23	24.30
BABEL-1	14.25	22.38
BABEL-2	15.09	27.52
BABEL-3	15.32	19.35
LARa / global	5.06	16.39
LARa / per recording	7.13	16.39


The reported conclusion is “does not transfer”: the strong HuGaDB result does not carry over to BABEL or LARa.
