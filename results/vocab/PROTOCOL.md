No outer evaluation scores were used to select the numerical correction; see `CONVERGENCE_PILOT.json`.

The classifier objective, five-value regularization grid, inner/outer splits, labels, representation definitions and seeds remain fixed. The MLP runs are unchanged. The extended L-BFGS-B check remained at its limit with a very small objective change. For small systems, the sensitivity therefore finishes with an exact-Hessian Newton refinement, fixing one redundant softmax intercept. Its Hessian is independently checked against finite differences of the gradient. Backtracking uses the training objective; the target maximum gradient is 1e-8. The first flagged fit reached 3.7e-11 in four Newton steps and differed from the primary fit on one held-out prediction. The direct-Hessian threshold was initially 1,000 free coefficients and expanded to 2,500 when a BABEL-1 K=16C fit was flagged, keeping the dense Hessian below 50 MB. This is a numerical sensitivity of the same classifier objective, not a new readout family or outer-score model selection.
