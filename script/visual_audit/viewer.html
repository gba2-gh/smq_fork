Provenance and interpretation limits

This view contains all evaluation recordings, including poor cases. Action colors are stable; raw state colors have no cross-model correspondence. The oracle display uses evaluation labels to merge states and is not a deployable method. No per-recording label remapping is performed. Motion replay is not included: a label timeline alone cannot tell whether a change is a meaningful movement phase, a subject cue, or sensor noise. Read recordings.csv and segments.csv for exact counts and intervals.
