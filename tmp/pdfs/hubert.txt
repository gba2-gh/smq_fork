   In this paper, we introduce Hidden unit BERT (HuBERT)
that benefits from an offline clustering step to generate noisy
labels for a BERT-like per-training. Concretely, a BERT model
consumes masked continuous speech features to predict pre-
determined cluster assignments. The predictive loss is only
applied over the masked regions, forcing the model to learn
good high-level representations of unmasked inputs to infer
the targets of masked ones correctly. Intuitively, the HuBERT
model is forced to learn both acoustic and language models
from continuous inputs. First, the model needs to model            Fig. 1: The HuBERT approach predicts hidden cluster assign-
